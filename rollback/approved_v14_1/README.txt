HOLY DIVER HD v14.1 — BRACELET PICKUP AND PAUSE MENU

1. Back up your current HD-pack folder.
2. Extract every file in this ZIP into the folder matching your ROM name.
   Example: Mesen/HdPacks/Holy_Diver_(Tr)/
   hires.txt must be directly in that folder, without another nested folder.
3. Enable NES HD packs. Fully close the ROM and reopen it to reload the pack.
4. Load your screen-2 save, or start normally.

The red winged-skull pickup is now a gold bracelet with a ruby. Its native
position, movement and item behavior are unchanged; the added wing animation
has been removed. After collection, Start shows the same bracelet in the pause
menu. Randy's shared normal and Twin Fire HD mappings include a
health-zero fallback for frozen death frames whose pose signatures fail.

All v13 images and the exact approved v12 audio patch are retained. The title
still starts clean: first Start reveals PRESS START, second Start begins play.
There is no replacement music or sound, and no change to gameplay or collision.
