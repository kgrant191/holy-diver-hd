HOLY DIVER HD 0.28.0 — DEVELOPMENT REVIEW CANDIDATE

Extends Level 6 through the green reliquary hall and the recorded tower
descent. Both retain the original green color families, with separate
skull-relief and shadowed tower backgrounds. Reuses the accepted green
stone, skull blocks, flying skulls, bronze crawlers, eye enemy and tears.
The small floating ledge retains its complete native outline and span.

Includes all prior Level 6 room variety, sentinel hit palettes, cape,
immediate-jump ceiling and 1-up repairs. Later unrecorded rooms and the
final boss remain unfinished.

Preserves approved artwork from earlier stages, native gameplay and audio.
This is a partial HD overhaul. Later unrecorded areas remain work in progress.
Version 1.0.0 is reserved for the completed, tested pack.

INSTALLATION
1. Back up your current HD-pack folder.
2. Extract every file directly into the folder matching your ROM name:
   Mesen/HdPacks/Holy_Diver_(Tr)/
   hires.txt must be directly in that folder, with no nested folder.
3. Enable NES HD packs. Fully close the ROM and reopen it to reload the pack.
4. Start a new game or load your existing state.
