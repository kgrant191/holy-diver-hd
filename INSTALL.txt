HOLY DIVER HD 0.3.0 — REVIEW CANDIDATE
First boss and Blizzard chamber. Main remains approved 0.2.1.

0.3.0 adds the horned boss, both firing postures, native hit/death flashes,
organ hall, bell chamber, Blizzard pickup and spell announcement. Shared
Randy/HUD sprites remain HD in the announcement and Stage 2 arrival.
Stage 2 scenery and enemies remain future work. This candidate is not approved.
This is a partial HD overhaul. Version 1.0.0 is reserved for the completed, tested pack.

0.2.1 replaces the sideways 1-up with a front-facing doll in both colors,
and joins the wall arches and columns into a continuous Gothic facade.

0.2.0 adds the fourth section: a Gothic tree court, miniature Randy 1-up,
shared HD ghouls, and native tile-bound branches, masonry and lower ground.

0.1.1 sharpens the standing ghoul while preserving all four rolling frames,
and adds complete crouching wind-up/release sprites for both Randy costumes.

0.1.0 adds a masonry chapel backdrop, emerald/ruby windows, reinforced blocks,
a gold serpent ornament, and all six observed purple-ghoul poses. Shared Randy,
weapon, HUD and defeat artwork continues across matching native identities.

The approved 0.0.2 corrections remove the rectangular title-prompt outline in both title states and
align both entrance shelves from the far-left standing area to the native fall edge.
The first Start reveals PRESS START; the second Start begins play.

1. Back up your current HD-pack folder.
2. Extract every file in this ZIP into the folder matching your ROM name.
   Example: Mesen/HdPacks/Holy_Diver_(Tr)/
   hires.txt must be directly in that folder, without another nested folder.
   The Actions artifact needs one extraction; it contains the pack files directly.
3. Enable NES HD packs. Fully close the ROM and reopen it to reload the pack.
4. Start a new game to see the stage introduction, or load your existing state.
