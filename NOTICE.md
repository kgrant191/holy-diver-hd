# Contents and provenance

Custom HD-pack artwork, mappings, scripts and records developed for this Holy Diver overhaul. Original game content and trademarks remain associated with their respective owners. No ROM or emulator is included.

Generated source images are retained in art/generated/. Registration metadata, available prompts and builder scripts document assembly. Approved snapshots remain in rollback/ for reproducibility.

The DejaVu font is accompanied by art/fonts/LICENSE-DejaVu.txt.

No general open-source license has been selected for this private project. Distribution and licensing can be decided separately before public publication.
