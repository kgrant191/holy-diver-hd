HOLY DIVER HD 0.31.0 — DEVELOPMENT REVIEW CANDIDATE

Completes the recorded red corridor and two purple rooms in Level 6.
The corridor has red brickwork and a crimson gallery; the purple rooms use
separate chapel and arcade backgrounds with native terrain contours.
Returning eyes, serpents and dragon pickup flashes reuse finished HD art.
Native movement, collision, timing, pauses, flashes and audio are unchanged.

Includes all approved 0.30.1 work, both bosses and the ending. The original
green enemy remains on standby. Version 1.0.0 is reserved for the completed,
tested pack after the remaining cleanup and approval.

INSTALLATION
1. Back up your current HD-pack folder.
2. Extract every file directly into the folder matching your ROM name:
   Mesen/HdPacks/Holy_Diver_(Tr)/
   hires.txt must be directly in that folder, with no nested folder.
3. Enable NES HD packs. Fully close the ROM and reopen it to reload the pack.
4. Start a new game or load your existing state.
