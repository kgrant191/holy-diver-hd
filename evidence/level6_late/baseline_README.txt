HOLY DIVER HD 0.27.0 — DEVELOPMENT REVIEW CANDIDATE

Level 6 rooms now follow their original red, amber and green color families
with new artwork. The opening exterior and hall retain their distinct
backdrops with red stone; the skull corridor uses an amber ossuary;
the vertical crawler chamber uses a crimson shaft; the next green room
uses a verdant crypt with blue accents. Platforms keep their native shapes.

Armored sentinels retain HD artwork in both hit palettes. The green room
also uses the finished actors, projectiles and bronze crawlers. Includes
the previous cape, immediate-jump ceiling and 1-up repairs.
Later Level 6 rooms and the final boss remain unfinished.

Preserves approved artwork from earlier stages, native gameplay and audio.
This is a partial HD overhaul. Later unrecorded areas remain work in progress.
Version 1.0.0 is reserved for the completed, tested pack.

INSTALLATION
1. Back up your current HD-pack folder.
2. Extract every file directly into the folder matching your ROM name:
   Mesen/HdPacks/Holy_Diver_(Tr)/
   hires.txt must be directly in that folder, with no nested folder.
3. Enable NES HD packs. Fully close the ROM and reopen it to reload the pack.
4. Start a new game or load your existing state.
